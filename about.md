# Background Video Menu 🎬

High-performance video & image background player with hardware acceleration, interactive mouse framing, and multi-scene customization for Geometry Dash.

- **Original Mod**: [lil2kki/Menu-Background](https://github.com/lil2kki/Menu-Background) by **lil2kki** (user95401).
- **Video Engine & UI Enhancements**: **Mituxs** & **lil2kki**.

---

### ✨ Features
- **🎬 Hardware Accelerated Video**: Plays `.mp4`, `.mov`, `.wmv`, `.webm`, `.avi` and `.m4v` files using Windows Media Foundation in background threads with zero lag.
- **🖼️ Image & GIF Support**: Works with standard `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.gif`, `.webp`, `.jxl`, and `.qoi` images.
- **🖱️ Interactive Mouse Framing**: Click **Adjust** to drag and position your background inside a 16:9 interactive viewport with your mouse.
- **⌨️ Editable Zoom**: Type your desired zoom percentage directly or use `+` / `-` buttons.
- **🎵 Independent Audio**: Keep Geometry Dash's normal soundtrack or play video audio.
- **📱 Multi-Scene Profiles**: Configure separate backgrounds for Main Menu, Shop, Vault, RobTop Levels, Online Browser, and Garage.
- **🧹 Hide Players & Particles**: Clean full-screen view with no jumping icons, trails, or dark overlays.
- **⚡ Instant Live Reload**: Changes apply immediately in-game.
